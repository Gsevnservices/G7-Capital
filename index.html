01 — Screening Note
Institutional-grade screening note
G7 Score 0–100 with PASS / WATCH / PRIORITY REVIEW verdict. Executive summary, thesis fit assessment, market, team, traction, competitive position, single point of failure identification, and deal-specific founder questions — calibrated to your firm's criteria on every deal.
02 — Growth & Return Dashboard
Growth quality and return modelling
Growth Quality Score (0–10) with decomposition across new logo vs expansion and organic vs paid. A 3×3 return scenario matrix across 3 exit multiples and 3 timelines. Breakeven analysis showing the exact ARR needed for 1x, 3x, and 5x — and the annual growth rate required to get there.

Risk scoring and opportunity calibration
Probability × impact scoring across binary, degradation, and external risks. Risk interaction and cascade modelling. The Risk-Adjusted Opportunity Score (RAOS) — one number integrating growth quality and risk into a conviction threshold. Monitoring checklist with trigger actions.
